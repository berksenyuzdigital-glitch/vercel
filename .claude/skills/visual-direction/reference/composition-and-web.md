## Website hero checklist

For every hero image, verify:
- Is there negative space for the headline?
- Is the CTA readable against what's behind it?
- Does the subject visually intrude behind the copy?
- Does the crop hold at both desktop and mobile?
- Does the main subject carry the right visual weight for the viewport?

## Website hero techniques

`Hero Composition`, `Product-Centered Hero`, `Off-Center Hero`, `Split Composition`, `Oversized Object`, `Cropped Object Composition`, `Object Breaking the Grid`, `Layered Depth`, `Foreground Occlusion`, `Full-Bleed Hero`, `Editorial Hero`, `Immersive Hero`, `WebGL Hero`, `3D Product Hero`, `Shader Background`, `Particle Field`, `Spatial UI`.

Bring the layout context into the prompt itself, e.g.: *"subject placed on the right third, clean negative space on the left for a two-line headline and CTA, no visually busy objects behind typography."*

## Product visualization decision order

Decide in this sequence, not all at once:

```
PRODUCT → HERO FEATURE → CAMERA → LENS → COMPOSITION
→ MATERIAL → LIGHT → BACKGROUND → COPY SPACE
```

Product visualization vocabulary: `Product Visualization`, `CGI Product Render`, `Product Rendering`, `Product Hero Render`, `Studio Render`, `Packshot`, `Beauty Shot`, `Key Visual (KV)`, `Lifestyle Render`, `In-Context Render`, `Floating Product Render`, `Product Anatomy`, `Component/Feature Visualization`, `Material Shot`, `Detail Shot`, `Feature Close-Up`, `Macro Product Render`, `Internal View`, `Transparent/X-Ray Product View`, `Product Breakdown`, `Assembly Visualization`, `Product Turntable`, `360 Product Render`.

## Camera ↔ website copy relationship

Answer these for every hero visual, not just "is it pretty":
- Where does the headline sit?
- Where does the CTA sit?
- Does the logo/nav collide with the subject?
- Where is the negative space?
- Does the subject's gaze/motion point toward the copy?
- What happens to the crop on mobile — does the subject disappear or get cropped badly?

A visual that's beautiful in isolation but fights the layout it has to live in has failed the brief.
